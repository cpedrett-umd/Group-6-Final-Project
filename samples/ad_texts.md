# Sample ad text

Copy any of these into the **Paste ad text** box (app) or the popup
(extension). One per tactic, plus a multi-tactic ad and a clean one.

Actual results are recorded in [README.md](README.md) — including the one that
gets it wrong.

---

**Urgency**

```
Act now! This offer ends tonight at midnight. Don't wait — sale ends soon.
```

**Scarcity**

```
Only 3 bottles left in stock. Limited supply, while supplies last. Exclusive access for early buyers.
```

**FOMO**

```
Everyone is switching to SmartHome Plus. Join thousands already using it — don't miss out on what you're missing.
```

**Fear appeal**

```
Protect your family from harmful bacteria. Your health may be at risk right now, and most people find out too late.
```

**Social proof**

```
Trusted by millions. Our top rated formula is a best seller with over 2 million satisfied customers worldwide.
```

**Authority**

```
Doctor recommended and scientifically proven. Backed by researchers, clinically tested, and certified by experts.
```

**Exaggerated claims**

```
Guaranteed results. Lose 20 pounds instantly with this miracle cure — a revolutionary breakthrough that works overnight.
```

**Several tactics at once** (the ad from the wireframe)

```
FINAL HOURS: Doctor-recommended MemoryMax Pro reverses memory loss in 14 days. Only 7 bottles left — 70% off before the supplement ban.
```

**A clean ad** (nothing pushy)

```
Corner Street Bakery is now open on Main Street, 7am to 3pm. Fresh sourdough daily. Visit our website for hours, directions, and our full menu.
```
